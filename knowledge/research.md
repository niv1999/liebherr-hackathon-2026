## 1. Liebherr-Hausgeräte

**Company facts**
- Part of the family-owned Liebherr Group. Production is at Ochsenhausen (DE) and Lienz (AT); the 2019 CR report lists three European production companies. An India appliance plant is also mentioned (details unverified). [Annual report 2025](https://www.liebherr.com/de-at/firmengruppe/geschäftsberichte/geschäftsbericht-2025/produktsegmente/kühl-und-gefriergeräte-8944601), [CR report 2019](https://www-assets.liebherr.com/media/bu-media/lhbu-hau/migrationsziel/corporate_responsibility_bericht_hausgeraete_2019_mig.pdf)
- Group HQ is Liebherr-International AG in Bulle, Switzerland (background knowledge, **[unverified]** in this session). Ochsenhausen is the appliance HQ. The German sales/service entity is Liebherr-Hausgeräte Vertriebs- und Service GmbH (named in the Bundeskartellamt release below).
- Segment revenue (refrigeration and freezing, Ochsenhausen + Lienz): **EUR 1,171 m in 2025 (+6.9%)**, after EUR 1,095 m in 2024 (-6.5%). Germany is the most important market. German demand kept falling, while Liebherr sales rose in the EU, especially Germany and France. [Annual report 2025](https://www.liebherr.com/de-at/firmengruppe/geschäftsberichte/geschäftsbericht-2025/produktsegmente/kühl-und-gefriergeräte-8944601)
- Headcount: about 6,596 in the prior year per the report page; Ochsenhausen alone about 2,000. About 1,350 staff were on short-time work at Ochsenhausen in early 2024. [Liebherr site profile](https://www.liebherr.com/de-de/firmengruppe/standort/ochsenhausen-profil-3705427), [Schwäbische](https://www.schwaebische.de/regional/biberach/ochsenhausen/liebherr-profitiert-vom-fokus-auf-das-eigene-zuhause-157282). Treat as approximate.
- Output is about 2.3 m appliances a year; Liebherr calls itself a leading European premium maker (self-description): [IFA exhibitor page](https://www.ifa-berlin.com/de/aussteller/liebherr-hausgerate-gmbh).

**Market position**
- Liebherr claims to be the best-selling brand in fridges and fridge-freezers in Germany in 2025 (NIQ GfK panel, unit sales). This is a manufacturer claim. [Liebherr](https://www.liebherr.com/de-de/gefrier-kuehlschraenke/ausgezeichnete-produkte-2451420)
- No independent German market-share figure was found. German fridge market revenue is about EUR 2 bn per Statista (paywalled): [Statista](https://de.statista.com/outlook/cmo/haushaltsgeraete/haushaltsgrossgeraete/kuehlschraenke/deutschland).
- The 2025 report cites growing Asian competition and private-label growth in Europe.

**Technologies (brand vocabulary)**
- BioFresh (separate low-temperature, humidity-controlled drawers), NoFrost, EasyFresh, SmartDevice app and SmartMonitoring (cloud), BluRoX full-vacuum insulation (German Sustainability Award for the FNXa 522i), VarioCool drawers, new French-door and modular built-in platforms. [Annual report 2025](https://www.liebherr.com/de-at/firmengruppe/geschäftsberichte/geschäftsbericht-2025/produktsegmente/kühl-und-gefriergeräte-8944601), [Innovations 2026 brochure](https://www.alleskueche.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Innovationen_2026.pdf)
- Series: **Pure, Plus, Prime, Peak** (entry to top). [Liebherr](https://www.liebherr.com/en-gb/fridges-freezers/series-fully-integrated-appliances-3011962)

**Model-name decoding** (source: the 2021 sales handbook [Liebherr VKHB 2021](https://www.alleskueche.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/Liebherr_VKHB_2021.pdf), read via search summary; the key may be outdated)
- First digit of the number maps to series: 0-1 Pure, 2-4 Plus, 5-6 Prime, 7-9 Peak.
- Letters: construction type (I = integrated, D = decor-ready, S = side-wall heating), product group (R = fridge, C = combination, F = freezer), features (B = BioFresh, N = NoFrost, D = drinks rack, P = pull-out).
- So "CBNsda 5223-22" reads as: C = combi, B = BioFresh, N = NoFrost, 5 = Prime series. The lower-case suffix "sda" and the "-22" generation/index digits are **not explained** in anything found. Guessing "sd = SmartDevice" is unconfirmed.
- Use for the tool: infer expected features (BioFresh, NoFrost) from the model name and check that the retailer page states them.

**News 2025-26 and digital/e-commerce**
- Liebherr runs its own online shop (appliances, accessories, spare parts, pickup at a specialist dealer). Appliance delivery is EUR 69 per device; payment by card, PayPal, Sofort, invoice (appliances only). [Shop FAQ](https://www.liebherr.com/de-de/gefrier-kuehlschraenke/shop/faq-2612376). So a D2C channel exists, a potential sensitivity with retailers.
- 2026 campaign: 360-degree "freshness" campaign (April to end of September 2026) with a free 10-year manufacturer warranty on selected appliances and a retailer sales contest. [Liebherr](https://www.liebherr.com/de-ch/n/starke-argumente-für-den-handel-liebherr-rückt-frische-und-10-jahre-garantie-mit-360-kampagne-in-den-fokus-255616-6661232). Warranty wording on retailer pages is something an audit can check.
- Retailer asset portal "Tradeplace" hosts campaign material, e.g. [BioFresh 2026 campaign PDF](https://downloads.liebherr.com/data/hau/lhv/eCom/Tradeplace_Portal/Liebherr_Kampagne_BioFresh_2026.pdf). The "EmpCo-Update" package was not found by search.
- IFA 2026 (Berlin, 4-8 Sept): more space, comfort, "intelligence". [Liebherr](https://www.liebherr.com/de-at/n/mehr-raum-mehr-komfort-mehr-intelligenz-liebherr-auf-der-ifa-2026-274880-4445452)
- The digitalization strategy mentions AI, chatbots and B2B webshops. [Annual report 2025](https://www.liebherr.com/de-at/firmengruppe/geschäftsberichte/geschäftsbericht-2025/produktsegmente/kühl-und-gefriergeräte-8944601)

**Key precedent: the LPR was reviewed by the Bundeskartellamt (2021)**
- Liebherr introduced the "Liebherr-Performance-Rabatt" in early 2021. Online criteria were stricter than offline ones: staff reachable 9:00-20:00 on Sundays and holidays, a guaranteed delivery period for out-of-stock items, and specific payment methods. Hybrid retailers could lose the rebate on store sales too. After the authority intervened, Liebherr aligned online with offline criteria and made availability more flexible; it also committed to giving refused retailers written reasons. [Bundeskartellamt](https://bundeskartellamt.de/SharedDocs/Meldung/EN/Pressemitteilungen/2021/12_04_2021_Liebherr.html?nn=55030)
- Implication: the audit tool must apply criteria equivalent to those for stores, be transparent, and be documented per retailer. This is the best "compliance by design" argument for a pitch.

## 3. Selective distribution and EU law (practical view)

Regulation (EU) 2022/720 (VBER) plus the Vertical Guidelines apply from 1 June 2022 for 12 years. [CMS](https://cms.law/en/deu/legal-updates/10-key-aspects-of-the-revised-eu-competition-law-in-the-field-of-distribution-vber-vgl2), [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2022/05/new-vber-and-guidelines-provide-clarification-for-online-sales-restrictions)

- A supplier may impose **quality criteria** on online sales in a selective system. The 2022 guidelines no longer require them to be identical to store criteria, but they must pursue the same legitimate aims and be proportionate. Commentators differ on how strict "equivalence" is; read the guideline text before relying on it. [Greenberg Traurig](https://www.gtlaw.com/it/insights/2022/6/the-new-vber-and-vertical-guidelines-explained-key-takeaways)
- **Hardcore restrictions** that lose the exemption include a total ban on selling online and a total ban on price-comparison tools. Restricting specific tools or marketplaces can be allowed. Marketplace bans are generally treated as allowable quality restrictions; a ban on a retailer's own shop or online advertising is risky. [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2022/05/new-vber-and-guidelines-provide-clarification-for-online-sales-restrictions)
- Hybrid platforms that compete with their sellers are excluded from the block exemption. [Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2022/05/new-vber-and-guidelines-provide-clarification-for-online-sales-restrictions)
- Practical takeaways for the tool:
  1. Score only criteria that are in the LPR contract and are objective and measurable. No price-based scoring.
  2. Apply identical criteria to online-only, hybrid and offline retailers (the 2021 Bundeskartellamt lesson).
  3. Keep an audit trail (evidence screenshot, URL, timestamp) so a retailer can contest a result.
  4. Do not derive pricing recommendations from the tool.
- Not legal advice; Liebherr legal should review the criteria.

## 4. EmpCo (Directive (EU) 2024/825) and energy label display

**EmpCo**
- Transposition deadline 27 March 2026; the rules **apply from 27 September 2026**, with no transition period. [Mayer Brown, Oct 2026](https://www.mayerbrown.com/ja/insights/publications/2026/10/the-eu-directive-on-empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-two-years-on-what-it-requires-and-where-the-member-states-stand), [Baker McKenzie](https://www.bakermckenzie.com/en/insight/publications/2026/08/european-union-final-call-the-eu-empco-directive). German transposition status was not checked here.
- Banned or restricted, in summary: generic environmental claims ("eco", "green", "environmentally friendly") without recognised excellent environmental performance; claims of neutral/reduced/positive impact based on offsetting; sustainability labels not based on a certification scheme or set by a public authority. [Coslaw](https://coslaw.eu/directive-eu-2024-825-generic-environmental-claims-and-practices-banned-in-the-eu-from-27-september-2026/), [Cooley](https://products.cooley.com/2026/03/16/empowering-consumers-for-the-green-transition-directive-check-your-sustainability-claims-and-warranty-information-for-compliance-with-new-eu-regime/). The directive also covers commercial-guarantee information (Cooley link). One source cites fines of up to 4% of turnover; **[unverified]**.
- Implication for retailer PDPs: the retailer is the trader on its page and answers for its own text, so copied manufacturer copy ("sustainable", "climate-friendly") can become an issue. A scannable check is a German/English phrase list ("umweltfreundlich", "nachhaltig", "klimaneutral", "öko", "grün", "energiesparend") plus unrecognised self-made eco badges. Report as a "risk flag", not a "violation", because context decides.
- The Liebherr "EmpCo-Update" asset package is not publicly documented; ask the challenge hosts what changed.

**Energy label online**
- Sellers must show label information close to the price: either the full label or the energy-class arrow (nested display) that links to the label and product information sheet; a link to EPREL is also accepted. [EU dealer guidance](https://energy-efficient-products.ec.europa.eu/dealers_da), [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/business/environment/energy-labels/index_en.htm)
- Refrigerating appliances fall under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2019/2016; Annex VIII covers distance selling. Exact layout rules were not verified. [Annex VIII (original text)](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2019/2016/annex/VIII/adopted)
- Auditable signals: arrow visible near the price, class letter matches EPREL, link to label and datasheet works, EPREL registration number present.

## 5. Returns for large appliances

- **No fridge-specific return data was found.** General German figures: about 10-11% of online purchases are returned across categories, driven mostly by fashion. Return cost per parcel is EUR 5-10 for 30% of surveyed retailers and EUR 10-20 for 26%; above EUR 20 for bulky items (retailer statements). [CIO/EHI](https://www.cio.de/article/3700440/retouren-kosten-im-schnitt-fuenf-bis-zehn-euro.html), [Händlerbund](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/themen/panorama/139201-so-hoch-kostenaufwand-retouren)
- Large appliances need a forwarder pickup, and a returned unit often cannot be resold as new. Brand programmes exist, e.g. Bosch's 30-day money-back guarantee with free collection. [Bosch](https://www.bosch-home.com/de/aktionen/geld-zurueck-garantie)
- Likely causes (my hypotheses, not data): does not fit the niche (width/height/depth, ventilation gap), door hinge side, built-in vs freestanding confusion, noise, capacity expectation, delivery damage, wrong variant, missing accessories. Most are product-data problems the tool can address. Ask Liebherr for their own return reasons.

## 6. AI readiness of product pages

**What the platforms officially say**
- Google: for AI Overviews and AI Mode there are **no additional requirements or special markup**, no AI-specific files and no special schema. Keep standard SEO hygiene, make important content available **as text**, match structured data to visible text, keep Merchant Center current. [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features)
- Google product markup: JSON-LD `Product` with sku, gtin, brand etc. [Merchant Center help](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/7331077?hl=en)
- OpenAI crawlers: **OAI-SearchBot** (ChatGPT search; allow it to be listed), **GPTBot** (training only), **ChatGPT-User** (user-initiated fetches; robots.txt may not apply). Settings are independent. [OpenAI docs](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots)
- Google-Extended is a robots.txt control token (Gemini); blocking it does not affect Search ranking; sources differ on AI Overviews scope. PerplexityBot indexes for Perplexity search. Secondary sources only: [overview](https://flaviocopes.com/robots-txt-ai-crawlers/)
- llms.txt: contested value, and Google says no AI-specific files are needed. Score at most as a low-weight optional signal.
- Agentic commerce (2026): OpenAI + Stripe **Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)**, published Sept 2025, relies on merchant product feeds and agentic checkout. Google **Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP)**, announced Jan 2026 with Shopify and partners, requires a Merchant Center account and feed for UCP checkout. [ppc.land](https://ppc.land/ucp/), [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/shopify-walmart-endorse-googles-new-open-commerce-protocol/), [inRiver](https://www.inriver.com/resources/acp-or-ucp-protocol-for-agent-commerce/). Coverage is mostly vendor blogging; official specs not checked. Frame as "readiness for", not "must have".

**Candidate checklist of measurable signals (per PDP, all scriptable)**
1. JSON-LD `Product` present and parseable; `name`, `brand` = Liebherr, `sku`/`mpn` = model number, `gtin13`, `image`, `description`.
2. `Offer`: `price`, `priceCurrency`, `availability` (schema.org enum), `url`; optionally shipping (`OfferShippingDetails`) and return policy (`MerchantReturnPolicy`).
3. Structured data matches visible text (price, availability, model).
4. Technical specs (dimensions, volume, energy class, noise dB, climate class) present **as HTML text/table**, not only in images or PDFs. Metric: share of required spec fields filled (about 15-20 key fields, compared with Liebherr's own data for that model).
5. Energy label arrow near price plus working link to label, datasheet or EPREL.
6. Raw HTML vs rendered DOM: content visible without JavaScript (many AI crawlers do not execute JS; **[unverified]** per vendor, so test it yourself).
7. FAQ/advice content (optional `FAQPage` markup) and a visible consultation route (phone, video).
8. Reviews: `AggregateRating` consistent with visible reviews.
9. Image alt texts, number and resolution of Liebherr images, video presence.
10. robots.txt: OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot and Googlebot allowed; GPTBot/Google-Extended is a business choice (record it, do not penalize). No blanket `Disallow: /`.
11. XML sitemap, canonical tag, HTTP 200, page speed and mobile (Lighthouse).
12. Low weight: llms.txt; Merchant Center feed (not externally visible, ask retailers).
13. Consistency of model number and GTIN with Liebherr master data.

Suggested score design: separate **LPR compliance**, **content quality** and **AI-readiness** sub-scores, each with evidence.

## 7. Existing tools and market

**Digital-shelf / content-compliance vendors**
- **Salsify** (PXM, syndication network, analytics), **Syndigo** (content creation, syndication, digital shelf analytics), **Profitero** (digital shelf analytics; integrates with Salsify/Syndigo). [Salsify](https://www.salsify.com/basic-item-syndication), [Profitero](https://www.profitero.com/products/automate), [Syndigo](https://syndigo.com/blog/universal-commerce-protocol-ai-shopping/)
- Other vendors were not researched. Verify before naming any.
- Positioning: these are generic enterprise products. A niche tool that encodes **Liebherr's specific LPR criteria** plus AI-readiness is differentiated. I found no vendor offering LPR-style rebate verification (absence of evidence only).

**Content syndication seen on retailer PDPs**
- **Loadbee**: installed once on the PDP; shows brand content (images, videos, A+ style) if the brand has content for the product, and renders nothing otherwise. Shopify and PrestaShop modules exist. [Shopify app](https://apps.shopify.com/loadbee)
- **Cliplister / DemoUp**: video and enhanced content syndication to 500+ shops via copy-paste code (competitor/vendor marketing claim). [DemoUp Cliplister](https://www.demoup-cliplister.com/?p=1365)
- **Tradeplace**: Liebherr's retailer asset portal (see BioFresh 2026 link in section 1). Whether it also syndicates live content was not confirmed.
- **How to detect (inference, confirm on real pages):** load the PDP in a headless browser and inspect network requests, script `src` and iframe `src` domains for `loadbee`, `cliplister`, `demoup`, `tradeplace`, `salsify`, `syndigo`, plus other common providers such as Flixmedia (my addition). Typical signs: an external script tag, an empty container that fills at runtime, or an iframe. Plain HTTP fetches miss these, so use a rendered DOM. Exact domains and selectors were not found in documentation; learn them from sample pages.
- Why it matters: the "Liebherr images/videos" criterion may be satisfied by syndication, not by the shop's own HTML.

## 8. So what for the hackathon

1. **Build an evidence pipeline, not a chatbot.** Crawl with a headless browser, extract signals, score, and keep screenshot/DOM evidence per criterion. The 2021 Bundeskartellamt case makes transparency and equal criteria a selling point.
2. **Use a rendered DOM** (syndicated content, JS-injected JSON-LD, lazy-loaded specs), and record raw-HTML visibility separately as an AI-crawler proxy.
3. **Parse the model name** (series digit, B/N letters) to predict what a PDP should say, then check the page. This ties content quality to returns (fit, features, hinge side, dimensions as text).
4. **Keep AI-readiness honest.** Google says no special markup is needed and llms.txt is unproven. Weight real signals (text specs, Product/Offer JSON-LD, GTIN, crawler access, no-JS content) and mark ACP/UCP as forward-looking bonus indicators.
5. **Add an EmpCo flag module** (generic green-claim phrases, unrecognised eco badges, warranty wording vs Liebherr's 10-year terms). The rules applied from 27 Sep 2026 and Liebherr has its own EmpCo asset update.
6. **Add energy-label checks** (arrow near price, link, class vs EPREL). Deterministic and legally grounded.
7. **Design for two readers:** an index and peer ranking for Liebherr staff, and a per-retailer fix list. Keep sub-scores separate so the LPR decision is not mixed with soft metrics.
8. **State data gaps openly in the pitch:** fridge return rates and white-goods online share are not public; ask Liebherr for their data rather than quoting guesses.
